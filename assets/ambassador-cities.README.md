# Orbit city pins

Counts come from the ambassador roster. City coordinates are from [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/), licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), with manual coordinates for a few locations not matched in the city dataset. Entries listing multiple or ambiguous locations remain in their country totals but are not assigned a city pin.

City pin records are editable in each Orbit project. Changing one does not change the country, regional, or worldwide totals.
